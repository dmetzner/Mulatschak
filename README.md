## About Mulatschak

Mulatschak is a traditional Austrian card game.

At the beginning of each round, you bid on how many tricks (*Stiche*) you think you can win. Then you have to prove it.

Play against up to **3 opponents** and try to win at least as many tricks as you announced.

If you succeed, your score goes down.
The first player to reach **0 points wins**.

Feeling confident? Announce a **Mulatschak** and try to win every trick in the round.
